Autocorrect fix contact packs

iOS learns spellings from your contacts. Import one of these packs and your iPhone stops "correcting" these names.

Pokémon names

All 1,025 Pokémon plus words like Pokédex, Sinnoh, Alolan and Gigantamax. Adds 14 contacts named "Dictionary - Pokemon 01" to "14".

Download .vcf

On iPhone: tap the link, then tap Add All Contacts. Updating later? Delete the old "Dictionary - Pokemon" contacts first.
