Autocorrect fix contact packs

iOS learns spellings from your contacts. Import one of these packs and your iPhone stops "correcting" these names.

These packs need Safari. Open this page in Safari

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone. Other browsers only show the first contact.
  2. Tap a pack's Download .vcf link. You'll see just the first contact.
  3. Tap the Send button, then choose Contacts.
  4. Tap Add All Contacts to add the whole pack at once.
  5. Restart your iPhone so the keyboard picks up the new words.
